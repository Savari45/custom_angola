🇦🇴 Angola - Accounting

Complete fiscal localization package for Angola
This module provides comprehensive accounting and tax compliance for companies operating in Angola.

Features

  • Chart of Accounts: Complete Portuguese chart of accounts following Angolan standards
  • Tax Structure: IVA (VAT) implementation with multiple tax rates (14%, 5%, 0%, exempt)
  • Fiscal Compliance: Tax reports and declarations for Angolan authorities
  • Multi-language: Portuguese (Angola) language support
  • Currency: Angolan Kwanza (AOA) support

Tax Rates

  • Standard IVA: 14% (most goods and services)
  • Reduced IVA: 5% (basic food items, books, medicines)
  • Zero Rate: 0% (exports, international services)
  • Exempt: Financial services, education, healthcare

Chart of Accounts Structure

  • 100000: Ativo (Assets)
  • 200000: Passivo (Liabilities)
  • 300000: Capital Próprio (Equity)
  • 400000: Receitas (Revenue)
  • 500000: Custos das Vendas (Cost of Sales)
  • 600000: Gastos por Natureza (Expenses by Nature)

Installation

This module will be automatically installed when the Accounting module is installed for companies based in Angola.
